01 · About Me

From Louisville to the Ocean State

I was born and raised in Louisville, Kentucky, which means two things are non-negotiable: I bleed red for the Cards, and I'll always call Kentucky home.

I grew up wrestling and playing football and baseball. Sports taught me a lot of what still shows up in how I sell: prepare, compete, and when you take a loss, reset and get after the next one. I took that up the road to Western Kentucky University, where I earned my BA in Business Administration in 2014.

My career started in sales at UPS and grew into telecom, cloud communications, and IT solutions. Along the way I met my wife in October 2016 and married her in October 2020. In January 2022 we packed up and moved to New England, and in November 2023 we put down roots in Rhode Island. Today we live in Johnston with our baby girl, who arrived in September 2025 and has firmly taken over as head coach of the household.

Professionally, I'm a consultative seller. I like walking into a messy situation, whether that's an aging phone system, a building with security gaps, or a print fleet nobody has looked at in years, and leaving with a plan the customer actually believes in.
